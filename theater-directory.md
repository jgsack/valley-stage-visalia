The intended coverage area is configurable:

- Local: 0-15 miles
- Regional: 15-35 miles
- Extended: 35-50 miles, including much of Fresno and Clovis

`Confirmed` means the organization was supplied directly by the project owner. `Research candidate` means it was found during the initial coverage search and still needs its current activity, official sources, and contact information verified.

## Public inclusion rule

An organization may appear publicly when at least one official or reliably attributable source provides one of the following:

- A future production or performance with a date
- A current season announcement
- A future audition notice
- A recurring annual season with a current schedule

Organizations without that evidence remain on the watchlist. An outside suggestion may nominate a missing organization, but agents must independently locate and verify its official sources before it qualifies for publication.

## Verification checklist

Before an organization is activated on the public website, record:

- Official organization name and any common aliases
- Producing organization separately from each physical venue
- Street address and geographic coordinates for each venue
- Straight-line distance from 93291
- Organization classification
- Current season or events page
- Ticketing page or platform
- Audition or participation page
- Public contact for reporting corrections or a missing organization
- Best automated collection method: API, structured page data, calendar feed, or page importer
- Last successful verification date

## Open coverage decisions

- Whether high-school productions should be included individually
- Whether concerts, dance, comedy, and film events at theatrical venues are in scope
- Whether the default visitor radius should be 30 or 50 miles
- Whether festivals should appear as organizations, collections of productions, or both
